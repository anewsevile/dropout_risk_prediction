problem.exe

CRITICAL_ALERT

/// THE CRISIS IN INDIAN HIGHER ED.

BARRIER 01

Socio-economic pressures push students out silently

First-generation learners, sudden family financial strain, commute fatigue, and academic isolation lead to gradual absenteeism. Students rarely reach out for help — they simply stop attending until re-admission becomes impossible.

BARRIER 02

No early warning system exists in most institutions

Colleges rely on fragmented paper attendance sheets and post-exam marksheets. By the time a student is officially designated as "detained" or "dropped out" at end-semester audits, the window for remediation has already closed.

🚨 NATIONAL STATISTICAL BENCHMARK:
10%+ dropout rates in certain regions (NIRF 2023)
